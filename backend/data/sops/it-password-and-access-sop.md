# TechNova Password Reset and Access Control SOP

## Password resets
The service desk may reset a forgotten password only after verifying the employee's identity with two factors: the registered mobile number and a manager confirmation. Resets requested by Phone must be called back on the registered number.

## Access reviews
Access for contractors expires after 90 days and must be renewed by their sponsoring manager. Access for staff who change teams is reviewed within 5 business days.

## Escalation
If a ticket is not triaged within twice its target, the shift lead must be notified. If an outage ticket remains unresolved after 8 hours, the IT Operations head is notified and a status update is sent to all affected teams every 2 hours.
