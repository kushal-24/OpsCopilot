# UrbanCart Refund Escalation and Peak-Period SOP

## When to escalate
Escalate a refund request when it has been in the same step longer than twice that step's target time, or when the customer contacts support a second time about the same request.

## How to escalate
1. The agent flags the case as High priority and notes the reason.
2. The team lead reviews the case within 4 hours.
3. If the delay is caused by a missing manager approval, the lead contacts the Head of Customer Operations directly.

## Peak periods
After holidays (mid-December to late January) return volume rises sharply. During this period RefundTeamA and RefundTeamB add a second review shift, and the approval target for managers is tightened from 24 hours to 12 hours.

## Customer communication
Customers receive a status email at Triaged, Approved and Resolved. Phone customers get a callback if the request exceeds the 48 hour target.
