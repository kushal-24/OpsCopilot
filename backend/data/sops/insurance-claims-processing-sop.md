# MediShield Health Claims Processing SOP

## Purpose
This SOP describes how a health insurance claim is processed from Submitted to Closed and the service targets for each stage.

## Stage targets
- Triaged (intake check by Ananya, Vikram or Sneha): within 4 hours. The check confirms the policy is active and documents are complete.
- In Review (ClaimsTeamA or ClaimsTeamB assessment): within 24 hours for standard claims.
- Approved (payout authorized by Manager_R or Manager_T): within 24 hours of the assessment.
- Resolved (payment issued): within 12 hours of authorization.
- Closed (file archived): within 24 hours of payment.
The end-to-end target is 72 hours. High priority claims should finish in 48 hours.

## Authorization thresholds
Claims up to $10,000 are authorized by a claims manager. Claims above $10,000 are escalated to a senior adjuster before manager authorization, and the approval target extends to 48 hours.

## Group policies
SMB and Enterprise group policies require confirmation from the policy administrator before payout. This confirmation must be requested during Triage, not after review.
