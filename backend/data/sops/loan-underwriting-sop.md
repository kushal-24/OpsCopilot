# GreenLeaf Loan Underwriting SOP

## Purpose
This SOP defines the stages and time targets for microfinance loan applications.

## Stage targets
- Triaged (document check by Lakshmi, Suresh or Farhan): within 1 business day.
- In Review (underwriting by UnderwritingTeamA or UnderwritingTeamB): within 3 business days.
- Approved (credit manager decision by Manager_M or Manager_V): within 2 business days of underwriting.
- Resolved (funds disbursed): within 1 business day of approval.
- Closed: after disbursement is confirmed.
The end-to-end target is 7 calendar days for personal loans and 10 calendar days for small business loans.

## Required documents
Every application needs proof of identity, proof of income or business turnover for 6 months, and a signed application form. SMB loans additionally require a business registration certificate. Missing documents pause the clock, and the applicant must be contacted within 1 business day.

## Credit manager authority
Manager_M may approve loans up to $5,000 and Manager_V up to $15,000. Larger loans need joint approval by both managers.
