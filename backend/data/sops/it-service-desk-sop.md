# TechNova IT Service Desk SOP

## Purpose
This SOP sets the service targets for IT support and access tickets raised by employees.

## Targets by priority
- High priority (outage, security, blocked executive): Triaged within 30 minutes, resolved within 8 hours.
- Medium priority: Triaged within 2 hours, resolved within 24 hours.
- Low priority: Triaged within 4 hours, resolved within 48 hours.

## Process stages
- Triaged: Meera, Karan or Tanvi categorize the ticket and assign it.
- In Review: ITSupportTeamA or ITSupportTeamB diagnoses the issue.
- Approved: Manager_D or Manager_P signs off only when the fix needs spend or elevated access. Routine fixes skip manager sign-off.
- Resolved: the fix is delivered and the employee is notified.
- Closed: the ticket closes automatically 48 hours after resolution unless the employee reopens it.

## Access requests
Software and VPN access requests need manager approval within 4 hours. Privileged or admin access also needs a security team check and is never approved over phone alone.
