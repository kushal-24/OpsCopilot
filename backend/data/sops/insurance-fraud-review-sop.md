# MediShield Claim Fraud and Quality Review SOP

## Red flags
A claim must be held for fraud review if any of these apply: the same procedure is claimed twice within 30 days, the claim is filed within 15 days of the policy starting, or the invoice does not match the provider records.

## Review process
1. The reviewing team marks the claim as on hold and notes the red flag.
2. The Special Investigations lead responds within 2 business days.
3. If cleared, the claim continues at In Review and its clock resumes. If not cleared, the claim is declined and the policyholder is notified in writing.

## Quality checks
Each week 5 percent of Closed claims are sampled and re-checked by a different team. If more than 2 percent contain an error, the team lead must run a retraining session within two weeks.
