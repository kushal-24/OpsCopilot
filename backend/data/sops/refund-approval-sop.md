# UrbanCart Refund Approval SOP

## Purpose
This SOP defines how refund requests move from Submitted to Closed at UrbanCart, and the time targets for each step.

## Step targets
- Triaged (eligibility check): within 4 business hours of submission.
- In Review (refund team verification): within 12 hours of triage.
- Approved (manager approval): within 24 hours of review completion.
- Resolved (money refunded): within 24 hours of approval.
- Closed: automatic once the refund is confirmed.
The overall target from Submitted to Closed is 48 hours for Low and Medium priority and 24 hours for High priority.

## Manager approval rules
Any refund above $500 requires approval from Manager_A or Manager_G. Refunds of $500 or less may be approved by the RefundTeam lead. If a manager has not acted within 24 hours, the request is escalated to the Head of Customer Operations.

## Eligibility
A request is eligible if it is submitted within 30 days of delivery and the item is unused or defective. Requests from Enterprise buyers for bulk orders need a written purchase order reference.
